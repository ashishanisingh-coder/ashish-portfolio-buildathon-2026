# V5.1 Flat Signal — QC and design review

**Date:** 2026-10-18
**Branch:** `v5.1-flat-art-direction`
**Preview:** `http://127.0.0.1:4184/v5.1/`
**Scope:** focused V5.1 refinement only. V5 remains the backup; production V3, V4, deployment and remaining case studies were not changed.

## What changed

V5.1 keeps the strongest parts of V5 — the fixed chapter header, dark/light contrast, strong opening typography and Philips-led narrative — while changing the visual grammar of the work scene:

- Removed rotated sheets, stacked paper, fake depth, 3D-style transforms and heavy shadows.
- Replaced the hero entrance with a single coordinated 2D sequence: clipped title-line reveals, rectangular image reveal, planar image settlement and editorial rules.
- Replaced the abrupt three-state Philips swap with a continuously interpolated scroll scene.
- Used one live Philips copy block that resolves and updates as the visitor moves through the scene, rather than stacking three competing narratives in the DOM.
- Renamed visitor-facing chapters to `Opening`, `Philips` and `Contact`.
- Added a continuously scroll-linked document progress marker, active chapter underline, readable progress readout and a mobile progress track/current-chapter state.
- Removed visitor-facing development commentary such as prototype, supplied-asset and unfinished-route language.
- Raised small metadata and footer text to improve readability at desktop and mobile sizes.

## Approved asset limitation

The approved project folder contains only one Philips-specific image:

`assets/actual/philips/case-study.jpg` — 1024 × 1536 composite.

It contains labelled panels for campaign overview, campaign-at-a-glance / print-social, catalogue, festive campaign, retail experience, format adaptations and takeaways. Separate high-resolution exports for the catalogue, print advertising, retail setup and format adaptations were not present. V5.1 therefore uses truthful crops of the single composite and does not invent files or aggressively upscale it.

The missing production-ready material for a richer Philips story is:

- individual high-resolution catalogue spreads;
- individual print-advertising exports;
- original retail setup / in-store photography at usable resolution;
- separate social and format-adaptation exports.

Those assets should be supplied before a production case-study page is built.

## Accessibility and motion

- Skip link targets `#main-content`.
- Native buttons and links retain visible focus rings.
- Desktop and mobile chapter controls expose `aria-current` correctly.
- Mobile menu exposes `aria-expanded`, `hidden`, and Escape-to-close behavior.
- The active Philips crop is exposed while non-active crops are hidden from assistive technology.
- Contact disclosure exposes `aria-expanded` and live status text.
- Reduced-motion mode keeps the overview beat visible, removes entrance/crop choreography and disables smooth scrolling.
- No horizontal overflow was found at 390 × 844.

## Critical comparison

### Allgood Studio interaction quality

**Improved toward the reference:** V5.1 now treats the header as a continuous instrument rather than a chapter switcher. The marker is tied to document scroll, the active label follows the viewport, and the mobile menu carries the same progress state. The Philips scene also has a longer, pinned-feeling read with an intentional visual sequence rather than repeated card-like states.

**Still behind the reference:** Allgood has a broader, more deeply choreographed chapter system and a denser page-long interaction language. V5.1 deliberately stops at three chapters because this is an approval gate, not a full-site rebuild. The next improvement should be more authored content and asset variation, not more effects.

### Metic Studio design precision

**Improved toward the reference:** the flat image window, restrained paper/ink palette, hairline rules, consistent sans voice, larger readable metadata and disciplined alignment create a more precise editorial surface. The Philips image is treated as a page of work, not a prop in a simulated presentation.

**Still behind the reference:** Metic benefits from a richer set of original project assets and a more complete typographic system. The single 1024 × 1536 composite limits crop fidelity and prevents V5.1 from showing genuinely separate catalogue, print and retail artifacts. That is an asset limitation, not a reason to manufacture imagery.

## Scope boundary

This is the requested V5.1 approval slice: refined navigation, final hero treatment, revised flat Philips storytelling, responsive mobile versions and motion evidence.
